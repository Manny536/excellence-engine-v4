# Dependency tower

```text
incidence geometry
  → Kakeya / Besicovitch tube geometry
    → Fourier restriction / l^2-decoupling
      → Dirichlet-polynomial large values
        → zeta growth / zero-density N(σ,T)
          → zero-free regions (bounds)
```

Every established rung outputs **bounds**, not exact critical-line placement.  
Lindelöf / Density Hypothesis sit under RH; reverse implications OPEN.

The geometric and restriction-facing rungs are grounded by Wang–Wu [P1], Wang–Zahl [P3],
and Guth–Wang–Zahl [P6]. These references support the named theorem background; they do not
establish the downstream arrows as a single theorem and do not close the exact bridge.
