# Exact zero-location burden

Required for any exact Ξ-realizing carrier:

```text
lengths  : log(p^k)
weights  : Λ(p^k) · p^(-k/2)
density  : archimedean Γ-factor with T log T counting
reality  : self-adjointness or adequate positivity substitute
output   : trace/determinant identity with multiplicity
```

Missing rung: non-circular map from placement/incidence to that carrier.

Poltoratski [P5] and ChaosBook Chapter 19 [P7] ground spectral-shift, trace, and determinant
vocabulary. Slater [P2] is retained only as a bounded Hilbert-Schmidt/determinant comparison.
None supplies the prime-carrying exact carrier specified above.
