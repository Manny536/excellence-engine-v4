# Evidence exposure protocol

Evidence exposure prevents HELD from becoming insulation.

## Procedure

1. Record at least one supporting item and one counter item.
2. Give each item an ID, a testable claim, and its current status.
3. Keep theorem background distinct from proposed bridges and live routes.
4. Confirm counterevidence can alter the formulation or trigger release.

## Pass / fail

Pass when both lists are non-empty, typed, and visible in the case trace. Fail when
support is offered without counterevidence, sources are merely named, or evidence is
present but cannot affect custody. The executable receipt is
`python probes/evidence_exposure_probe.py`.
