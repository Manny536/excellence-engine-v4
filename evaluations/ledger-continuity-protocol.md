# Ledger continuity protocol

Continuity makes ownership and downstream status inspectable.

## Required ownership

- Excellence Engine V4: custody architecture and evaluation surface;
- KakeyaLogic: geometric field and controlling Outcomes artifacts;
- Claude V6: theorem-facing ledger;
- LoveLabs-LCA: CUP / BD-AI study records;
- Grok Terminal: separate polarized reading;
- PeAIce Outcomes: public downstream surface.

## Pass / fail

Pass when `SOURCE_MAP.md` names every surface with an explicit URL and the case trace
contains at least two source pins. Fail on an orphaned artifact, silent ownership
transfer, or cross-model consensus used as confirmation. Run
`python probes/ledger_continuity_probe.py`.
