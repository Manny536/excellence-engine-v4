## Multiscale continuation gate

[`multiscale-compactness-gate.md`](multiscale-compactness-gate.md) represents simultaneous
scale and configuration refinement by a product-directed net. In this binding, `L²_C` means a
checkable continuation relation across refinements. It does not acquire standard Hilbert-space
properties from the symbol.

The gate permits a limit candidate only after a compact carrier is established. Retaining the
candidate as coherent then requires a separately proved closed continuation condition. A weakly
compact `L²` realization is a conditional analytic lane and remains `OPEN` for framework `L²_C`.
