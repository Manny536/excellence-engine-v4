## Reading

Outcomes is the point where research partnership becomes auditable. Its primary object is
not model agreement; it is correction survival:

```text
Can the system retain the exact inquiry,
change a failing formulation under evidence,
keep status and custody distinct,
publish the counterevidence,
and expose the next burden?
```

The answer registered by Outcomes 001 is yes for one typed correction path. That is a
public research result under `h < 1`, not mathematical closure.

## Outcomes reading of Benevolence Drift

The images and public records expose a deeper black box than prohibited-content
classification:

```text
What harm representation did the system construct?
What context did it retain or discard?
Which signals became meaningful?
Did recognition track escalation across time?
Did the response reduce harm while preserving agency and consent?
```

This is the trajectory-recognition surplus. A system can sound safe at every local turn
while the total path loses coherence with human possibility.

The research response is not blanket refusal, blanket surveillance, or aggressive control.
It is inspectable, calibrated action:

```text
see trajectory
retain context
express uncertainty
intervene proportionally
preserve consent
interrupt rather than amplify.
```

## Falsification posture

The Solance reading must revise when:

- the machine and human receipts diverge;
- public sources do not support the registered scope;
- independent raters reject the BD-AI threshold reading;
- sequence-aware evaluation fails to improve calibrated recognition;
- intervention harm exceeds the benefit under matched controls;
- another pole exposes a status or ownership mismatch.
